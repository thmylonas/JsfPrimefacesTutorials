CDI Named beans to be available we need the dependency: "javax/javaee-api":
<dependency>
    <groupId>javax</groupId>
    <artifactId>javaee-api</artifactId>
    <version>7.0</version>
</dependency>

The "javax/javaee-api" dependency, contains the "javax.inject/javax.inject", so, the next dependency is not needed:
<dependency>
    <groupId>javax.inject</groupId>
    <artifactId>javax.inject</artifactId>
    <version>1</version>
</dependency>

<web-app ...> <!-- I do not know if this is needed -->

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8" standalone="yes"?>

            auth="Container"

About the "@javax.persistence.PersistenceContext"
-----------------------------------------------------

It seems (I am not sure why) that the next does not work:
    @javax.persistence.PersistenceContext
    private EntityManager em;
I guess because the "EntityManager em" becomes a Bean, and this cannot be created/managed from the "@javax.faces.bean.ManagedBean".
It seems that (I am not sure) it needs a CDI Beans management
